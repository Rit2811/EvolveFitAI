# Project Brief

## Product
**EvolveFit AI** is a personalized AI fitness and nutrition coach for general wellness. It creates a weekly workout and meal plan from user biometrics, goals, schedule, equipment, diet, and allergies. A daily check-in captures recovery and adherence, after which the plan can be adapted with a clear explanation.

## Core user story
As a user, I can enter my profile and preferences, receive a tailored weekly plan, report how I feel, and get a safer, more practical updated plan.

## Main demo story
1. Register and sign in.
2. Complete onboarding.
3. Generate an initial plan.
4. Verify equipment and dietary constraints.
5. Submit a low-sleep/high-soreness check-in.
6. Adapt the plan.
7. Show reduced intensity or recovery changes.
8. Show why the plan changed.
9. View dashboard and progress data.
10. Ask the AI coach to explain the change.

## Non-goals
- Medical diagnosis or treatment
- Injury rehabilitation
- Exact clinical nutrition
- Wearable integrations
- Social network
- Payments
- Trainer marketplace
- Production-scale infrastructure

## Disclaimer
EvolveFit AI provides general fitness and nutrition guidance and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or professional treatment.
